Assumptions

Pressure: Standard atmosphere from elevation unless manual barometer is checked. Loads: Coil load uses mixed air to leaving-coil air. Fan gain adds downstream sensible heat for the supply-after-fan zone load. Wet bulb: Thermodynamic estimate found by matching saturated-air enthalpy at the same pressure. Field note: Check sensor placement, airflow, economizer position, and equipment fan heat when comparing to measured capacity.
